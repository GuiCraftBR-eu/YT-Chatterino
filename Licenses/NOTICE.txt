YT-Chatterino 2.0.2 — native library notices

The application dynamically links unmodified Qt 6.8.3 Core/Gui/Widgets/Network/Svg
and Qt image/platform/TLS plugins. Qt is used under LGPL 3.0; the complete
upstream license texts and bundled third-party notices are included in the
qtbase/qtsvg/qtimageformats notices. The SBOM files identify the shipped builds.

Qt source archives, matching the Windows binaries, are available from:
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/submodules/qtbase-everywhere-src-6.8.3.tar.xz
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/submodules/qtsvg-everywhere-src-6.8.3.tar.xz
https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/submodules/qtimageformats-everywhere-src-6.8.3.tar.xz
They are also distributed with this release as a separate library-sources asset.
No Qt library changes were made. Users may replace the shared libraries with
compatible modified builds and debug those modifications, including reverse
engineering needed for that purpose. Rebuild instructions and application source
are in the repository. No library replacement is blocked by the updater or app.
